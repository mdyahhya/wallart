Return & Refund Policy

100% replacement guarantee for Transit Damages.

1. Damaged or Defective Items

Every Walltone piece is hand-crafted and thoroughly quality checked before packing. If your item arrives damaged during transit, please inform us within 48 hours of receipt with unboxing photos/videos.

2. Replacement Process

Upon verifying the damage details, we will arrange a free replacement of the damaged piece or dispatch a new unit immediately at no extra cost to you.

3. Cancellation & Refunds

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch for a full refund. Refunds for eligible cancellations are credited back to your original payment method via Cashfree within 5–7 business days.
