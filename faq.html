Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Walltone Decors.

Q: What material is used in Walltone Decors products?

All our products are crafted from premium high-grade mild steel / metal with anti-rust powder coating and hand-finished acrylic/gold detailing for long-lasting durability.

Q: How do I install the wall art or clocks?

Every piece features built-in hanging hooks on the reverse side. Wall mounting hardware (screws and anchors) is included in your delivery box for quick 2-minute hanging.

Q: How long will delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 6–7 business days across India. You will receive tracking notifications as soon as your order is dispatched.

Q: Can I preview how an art piece looks on my wall before buying?

Yes! Use our interactive AI Cam tool to visualize any wall art directly on your wall in real-time using your phone or laptop camera.
